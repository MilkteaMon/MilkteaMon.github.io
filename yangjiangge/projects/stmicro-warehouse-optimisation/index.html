AI-Powered Warehouse Put-Away Optimization

An ongoing student consultancy project exploring AI-supported storage decisions in warehouse put-away operations.

In progress

Warehouse operations

August 2026 – April 2027 (expected)

01 / Context

The problem

Put-away determines where incoming inventory should be stored. This decision connects available storage space, material movement and the effort needed to retrieve items later.

02 / Purpose

The objective

Explore how AI and optimisation can support the choice of storage locations and make warehouse put-away decisions more systematic.

03 / Progress

Current status

In progress

Ongoing industry collaboration with STMicroelectronics, from August 2026 to April 2027 (expected). The project focuses on warehouse put-away optimisation.

04 / Ownership

My contribution

Student Consultant in an industry collaboration focused on AI-powered warehouse put-away optimisation.

05 / Method

Decision framework

  • Inputs: incoming inventory and the storage locations available for consideration.
  • Decision: where to place an item, considering space and movement tradeoffs.
  • AI support: explore assistance for this decision workflow alongside operational constraints.

06 / Looking ahead

Limitations & next steps

  • Potential extension: compare storage policies using movement, space use and retrieval effort as evaluation dimensions.
